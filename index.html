Starting a Reunion

  1. Pick a year and a date

    Get a few friends together and choose a weekend.

  2. We help with the rest

    The alumni team can help with invitations and the building.

  3. Come back and see IA

    Look round the academy and catch up.

Mentoring

Help a current student with what comes after Year 11.

How It Works

  1. Tell us about you

    Your job or course, and how you got there.

  2. We find a match

    We pair you with a student you can help.

  3. Meet a few times a year

    At IA or online. Mentors complete safeguarding checks first.

Ways to Help

  1. Speak at a careers evening

    Tell Year 10 and 11 what your job is really like.

  2. Host work experience

    Take a student for a day or a week.

  3. Run mock interviews

    Help Year 11 practise with someone who has been in the room.
